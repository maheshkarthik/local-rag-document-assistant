# Quillmark Analytics 2027 US Benefits Summary
> Plan year January 1 to December 31, 2027. Open enrollment runs November 9 to November 25, 2026. Fictional company and fictional insurance plans; for testing only.

## 1. Who Is Eligible

All US employees scheduled to work 30 or more hours per week are eligible from their first day of employment. You may also enroll your spouse or domestic partner and children up to age 26. Canadian employees receive a separate benefits guide.

During open enrollment you can change medical, dental and vision plans, add or remove dependents, and set your FSA or HSA contributions. Outside open enrollment you can only make changes within **30 days** of a qualifying life event such as marriage, birth or adoption, or loss of other coverage.

### 2.1 Monthly employee contributions

Quillmark pays 100% of the employee-only premium for the Basic HDHP and most of the cost of the other plans. Your monthly payroll deductions are:

| Coverage level | Basic HDHP | Standard PPO | Premium PPO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employee only | $0 | $48 | $112 |
| Employee + spouse/partner | $96 | $210 | $338 |
| Employee + child(ren) | $82 | $184 | $296 |
| Family | $165 | $342 | $520 |

These amounts increase by about 4% from 2026. The Premium PPO increased the most (7%) because of higher claims.

## 3. Health Savings Account (HSA)

If you enroll in the Basic HDHP, Quillmark contributes to your HSA every pay period:

- **$1,000 per year** for employee-only coverage
- **$2,000 per year** for any coverage that includes dependents

You may add your own pre-tax contributions up to the 2027 IRS limit, minus the Quillmark contribution. Employees aged 55 or older may make an additional catch-up contribution. HSA balances are yours to keep if you leave Quillmark.

## 4. Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)

- **Health care FSA:** available with the Standard and Premium PPO plans. You may carry over up to the IRS carryover limit into 2028.
- **Dependent care FSA:** up to $7,500 per household per year for eligible child or elder care, available with any plan.

FSA funds are use-it-or-lose-it beyond the carryover amount. Claims for 2027 expenses must be submitted by March 31, 2028.

## 6. Life and Disability Insurance

- Basic life insurance of **2x annual salary** (up to $750,000), paid by Quillmark.
- Optional supplemental life insurance up to 5x salary, at employee cost.
- Short-term disability: 70% of salary for up to 12 weeks, paid by Quillmark.
- Long-term disability: 60% of salary up to $15,000 per month after 90 days, paid by Quillmark.

## 7. Retirement: 401(k)

- You are automatically enrolled at **4%** of eligible pay 30 days after your start date. You can change or opt out at any time.
- Quillmark matches **100% of the first 4%** you contribute and **50% of the next 2%**, for a maximum match of 5% of eligible pay.
- Matching contributions vest over three years: 33% after one year, 66% after two years and 100% after three years. Your own contributions are always 100% vested.
- Roth and traditional pre-tax options are both available.

## 8. Other Benefits

- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** six free, confidential counselling sessions per issue per year, plus legal and financial consultations. Call 1-800-555-0199, available 24 hours a day.
- **Wellness reimbursement:** up to $50 per month for gym memberships, fitness classes or meditation apps.
- **Commuter benefits:** pre-tax transit and parking accounts.
- **Fertility and family-forming benefit:** up to $20,000 lifetime for fertility treatment, adoption or surrogacy (new in 2027).
- **Pet insurance:** voluntary, paid by the employee.

## 9. New in 2027

1. Fertility and family-forming benefit added.
2. HSA employer contribution increased from $750 to $1,000 (employee only) and from $1,500 to $2,000 (with dependents).
3. Dental Plus orthodontic lifetime maximum raised from $1,500 to $2,000.
4. The Standard PPO specialist copay increased from $45 to $50.

## 10. How to Enroll

Log in to Workday, open the Benefits app and select "Open Enrollment 2027". If you take no action, you will keep your current medical, dental and vision elections, but **FSA elections do not roll over** and must be made again each year. Questions: benefits@quillmark.example.

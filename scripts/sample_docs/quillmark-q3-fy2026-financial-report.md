# Quillmark Analytics: Q3 FY2026 Quarterly Report
> Quarter ended September 30, 2026. Prepared by the Finance team for the Board of Directors. Fictional company; all figures are invented.

## 1. Highlights

- Revenue was **$18.4 million**, up 27% from $14.5 million in Q3 FY2025 and up 6% from $17.3 million in Q2 FY2026.
- Annual recurring revenue (ARR) reached **$71.2 million**, up 31% year over year.
- Net revenue retention was **118%**, compared with 112% a year ago.
- Gross margin improved to **76.6%**, from 73.1% in Q3 FY2025, mainly because of lower cloud hosting costs per customer.
- Operating loss narrowed to $1.1 million, from $3.4 million a year earlier.
- The company signed 14 new customers, including FreshWay Markets, its largest contract to date (3 years, $4.8 million total contract value).
- Cash and short-term investments were $42.6 million at quarter end.

Quillmark's fiscal year runs from January 1 to December 31, so Q3 FY2026 covers July 1 to September 30, 2026.

### 3.2 By region

The United States contributed $15.1 million (82%) and Canada $3.3 million (18%). Canadian revenue grew 44% year over year following the opening of the Toronto office in February 2026.

### 3.3 Products

The core product, **Quillmark Forecast**, accounted for 71% of subscription revenue. **Quillmark Shelf**, the inventory optimization add-on launched in Q4 FY2025, reached 58 customers and $2.9 million in quarterly revenue. The new **Waste Insights** module entered beta with 9 customers and is expected to be generally available in Q1 FY2027.

## 5. Cash Flow and Balance Sheet

- Operating cash flow was positive at **$0.9 million**, the second consecutive positive quarter.
- Capital expenditure was $0.4 million, mainly laptops for new hires and the Toronto office fit-out.
- Free cash flow was $0.5 million.
- Deferred revenue rose to $29.7 million, from $24.1 million a year earlier, reflecting more annual upfront billing.
- The company has no debt. An undrawn $10 million revolving credit facility with Cascade Commercial Bank remains available until 2028.

## 6. Outlook for Q4 FY2026

Management expects Q4 FY2026 revenue between **$19.2 million and $19.6 million** and an operating loss between $0.4 million and $0.8 million. Full-year FY2026 revenue is expected to be between $71.0 million and $71.4 million, up from $55.9 million in FY2025.

Management targets operating break-even in Q2 FY2027. Key risks include grocery sector consolidation, slower-than-expected adoption of Waste Insights, and rising hosting costs if the planned migration to reserved cloud capacity (Project Atlas) slips.

## 7. Headcount Plan

The company plans to hire 22 people in Q4, mostly in engineering (12) and customer success (6), bringing year-end headcount to approximately 360. Hiring for G&A roles is paused until Q1 FY2027.

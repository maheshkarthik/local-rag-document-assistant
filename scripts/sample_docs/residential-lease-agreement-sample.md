# Residential Lease Agreement (Sample)
> Sample only, with fictional parties and property. Not legal advice and not valid for use as a real lease.

## 1. Parties

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made on **June 12, 2026** between **Harborview Property Management LLC** ("Landlord"), 410 Commerce Street, Suite 200, Riverton, and **Jordan Ellis and Sam Whitaker** ("Tenants"). Each Tenant is jointly and severally responsible for all obligations under this Lease.

## 2. Property

The Landlord leases to the Tenants the residential unit at **1827 Maple Crest Avenue, Apartment 3B, Riverton** ("Premises"), a two-bedroom, one-bathroom apartment, together with one assigned parking space (space number 14) and storage locker B-7. The Premises are provided with a refrigerator, stove, dishwasher and in-unit washer and dryer.

## 3. Term

The Lease begins on **July 1, 2026** and ends on **June 30, 2027**. If the Tenants remain in possession after the end date with the Landlord's consent and no new lease is signed, the tenancy continues month to month under the same terms, except that rent increases by 5%. Either party may end a month-to-month tenancy with 60 days' written notice.

## 4. Rent

- Monthly rent is **$2,150**, due on the **1st day** of each month.
- Rent is paid through the Harborview online portal or by check payable to Harborview Property Management LLC.
- Rent for the first month is due when the Lease is signed.
- If rent is not received by the end of the **5th day** of the month, a late fee of **$75** is charged, plus **$10 per day** after that, up to a maximum of $175 per month.
- A fee of $35 is charged for any returned payment. After two returned payments, the Landlord may require payment by certified funds.

## 5. Security Deposit

The Tenants pay a security deposit of **$3,225** (one and a half months' rent) before moving in. The Landlord will hold the deposit in a separate account and return it, minus any lawful deductions, within **21 days** after the Tenants move out and return the keys. Deductions may be made for unpaid rent, cleaning to return the unit to move-in condition, and damage beyond normal wear and tear. The Landlord will provide an itemized list of deductions. The deposit may not be used by the Tenants as the last month's rent.

## 6. Utilities

| Utility | Paid by |
|---|---|
| Water, sewer and trash | Landlord |
| Electricity | Tenants |
| Gas (heating and stove) | Tenants |
| Internet and cable | Tenants |

## 7. Occupants and Guests

Only the Tenants named above may live in the Premises. Guests may stay for up to **14 days in any six-month period**; longer stays require the Landlord's written consent.

## 8. Pets

One cat or one dog under 35 pounds is allowed with the Landlord's written approval, a one-time non-refundable pet fee of **$300**, and monthly pet rent of **$40**. The Tenants are responsible for any damage caused by the pet. Dangerous or exotic animals are not permitted. Service and assistance animals are not considered pets and are not subject to pet fees.

## 9. Use, Maintenance and Repairs

- The Tenants must keep the Premises clean and report any needed repairs promptly through the maintenance portal.
- The Landlord is responsible for repairs to plumbing, heating, electrical systems, appliances supplied by the Landlord, and the structure.
- Emergencies such as flooding, no heat in winter, or gas smells must be reported immediately by phone to the 24-hour maintenance line: (555) 014-2290.
- The Tenants must replace smoke detector batteries and must not disable smoke or carbon monoxide detectors.
- The Tenants may not paint, install fixtures or make alterations without written consent. Small nail holes for hanging pictures are considered normal wear and tear.

## 10. Landlord's Entry

The Landlord may enter the Premises to inspect, make repairs or show the unit to prospective tenants or buyers, after giving at least **24 hours' written notice**, between 8:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. In an emergency the Landlord may enter without notice.

## 11. Quiet Hours and Smoking

Quiet hours are from 10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. Smoking of any substance, including vaping, is prohibited inside the Premises, on balconies and within 25 feet of building entrances.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 12. Subletting

The Tenants may not sublet the Premises or assign this Lease without the Landlord's prior written consent. Short-term rentals (for example through vacation rental websites) are prohibited.

## 13. Early Termination

The Tenants may end this Lease early by giving **60 days' written notice** and paying an early termination fee equal to **one month's rent** ($2,150). Tenants who are called to active military duty may end the Lease as permitted by law without paying the fee.

## 14. Renters Insurance

The Tenants must keep renters insurance with at least **$100,000** of personal liability coverage throughout the term, and provide proof of coverage before move-in. The Landlord's insurance does not cover the Tenants' belongings.

## 15. Move-Out

At least 30 days before moving out at the end of the term, the Tenants must give written notice. The Tenants must return all keys, fobs and parking permits, remove all belongings and leave the Premises clean. A move-out inspection will be scheduled with the Tenants, who may attend.

## 16. Signatures

Tenants: Jordan Ellis, date June 12, 2026. Sam Whitaker, date June 12, 2026.
